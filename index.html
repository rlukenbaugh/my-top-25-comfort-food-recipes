Freezer Guide at a Glance

Small habits that protect flavor and texture.

Cool completely

Divide food into shallow containers and refrigerate promptly before freezing.

Remove excess air

Use tight containers or freezer bags with as much air pressed out as possible.

Label and date

Write the dish name, freeze date, and any finish-at-serving instructions.

Reheat fully

Thaw in the refrigerator when practical and reheat leftovers to 165 F.
